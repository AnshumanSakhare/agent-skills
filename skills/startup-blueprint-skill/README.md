> Turns a startup, repo, site or product idea into an evidence-backed business plan: monetisation strategy, tier and pricing architecture, an editable formula-driven 12-month financial model, unit economics and break-even under explicit assumptions, plus a 90-day execution roadmap.

## Install

```bash
npx github:AnshumanSakhare/agent-skills add startup-blueprint-skill
```

<sub>Installs into every agent directory found on your machine. Target one explicitly with `--client claude` &middot; `codex` &middot; `cursor` &middot; `opencode` &middot; `project`, or drop it anywhere with `--dir <path>`.</sub>

## What it does

- Decides free vs paid, and monthly vs annual vs usage vs one-time vs lifetime
- Designs tiers and the upgrade triggers between them
- Builds a real .xlsx model with live formulas you can edit, not a static table
- States every assumption explicitly and lets you compare scenarios
- Estimates unit economics and break-even honestly
- Ships a 90-day roadmap as CSV you can import

## Try it

Once installed, just talk to your agent in plain language:

> *"Build a business plan and pricing model for https://example.com."*

> *"Should this product be usage-based or seat-based? Model both."*

> *"Give me a 12-month financial model and a 90-day roadmap for my SaaS idea."*

## What you get back

- ✅ &nbsp;**Markdown business plan**
- ✅ &nbsp;**Formula-driven .xlsx model**
- ✅ &nbsp;**90-day roadmap CSV**
- ✅ &nbsp;**HTML report**
