> Takes a loose company or product brief and derives an unconventional but coherent art direction from the product's actual truth — material, ritual, origin, contradiction — then builds a working storefront around it with original ImageGen product imagery, cart interactions and validation.

## Install

```bash
npx github:AnshumanSakhare/agent-skills add crazy-ecommerce-builder-skill
```

<sub>Installs into every agent directory found on your machine. Target one explicitly with `--client claude` &middot; `codex` &middot; `cursor` &middot; `opencode` &middot; `project`, or drop it anywhere with `--dir <path>`.</sub>

## What it does

- Derives a creative thesis from product truth, not from adjectives like 'modern' or 'premium'
- Locks a continuity system so every generated product image belongs to the same world
- Builds a real storefront: browse, product detail, variants, cart, checkout state
- Holds a commerce checklist so the experimental interface stays shoppable
- Keeps navigation native and controls keyboard-reachable
- Optionally publishes the finished store

## Try it

Once installed, just talk to your agent in plain language:

> *"Build a store for a ceramics studio that fuses pottery and glass into one cup. Make it strange."*

> *"Turn this brand brief into an ecommerce site with original product photography."*

> *"Redesign my Shopify landing experience as something nobody would guess came from a template."*

## What you get back

- ✅ &nbsp;**Working responsive storefront**
- ✅ &nbsp;**Original generated product imagery**
- ✅ &nbsp;**Documented creative thesis**
